Game Lights

Your Hue lights flash in your team's colours when it scores, green when it takes the ball away, and red when it turns it over. It follows the teams people pick on the Sports page while their games are on. Who it follows, the TV delay and brightness are under Admin → Plugins → Game Lights → Settings. Each person can also pick their own room or zone, delay and team colours on My Game Lights (on their Account page) or in the TV app's Sports tab; the lights ticked here are for people who don't pick a room.

Lights

Effects

Untick an effect to skip it. Test plays it now on the chosen lights. Football follows each play; hockey and soccer flash for goals, baseball for runs, and basketball only for a win.

Team colours

Colours come from ESPN, with black and white swapped out because they make poor flashes. Pick your own if a team's look wrong on your bulbs.
