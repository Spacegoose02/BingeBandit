Watchlist feeds

Share your watchlist as an RSS feed, or subscribe to feeds of movies and shows to fill it.

Your watchlist as a feed

Feed readers and other apps can't sign in, so the address needs one of your API keys. Make one under Account → API keys and put it in place of YOUR_API_KEY.

Subscribed feeds

Each feed is checked regularly. Anything in it that's in the library goes on your watchlist; the rest is tried again on each check, so it's added once it arrives. Items with TMDB ids (Letterboxd-style feeds) match best; otherwise titles like "Title (2019)" are matched by name and year.
