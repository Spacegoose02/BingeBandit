Hue Sync

Your lights follow the colours on screen while something plays. Choose what each light follows; the rest (who and which devices, brightness, delay) is under Admin → Plugins → Hue Sync → Settings.

Bridge

Paired with the bridge at .

Find the bridge's address in the Hue app (Settings → My Hue system → the bridge → ⓘ), or try Find. Then press the round link button on top of the bridge and click Pair within 30 seconds.

Lights

Point each light at the part of the screen nearest it: a lamp left of the TV follows the left edge, and so on. Lights left on "Not used" are never touched.

Each device can keep to one room or zone from the Hue app (on the web: Account → Playback; on the TV: Settings → Hue lights), so TVs in different rooms each sync their own lights at the same time.
